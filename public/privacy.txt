PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: January 2026

This privacy policy explains how we handle data when you use our application and connect your Oura and/or Garmin accounts.

1. DATA COLLECTION

When you authorize our application, we access data from your connected accounts:

Oura Ring Data:
- Sleep metrics (duration, phases, efficiency, timing)
- Readiness scores and contributors
- Activity data (steps, calories, active time)
- Biometric data (heart rate, HRV, temperature, SpO2)

Garmin Connect Data:
- Activity and workout summaries
- Daily metrics (steps, heart rate, stress, sleep)
- Health stats and body composition
- Training and recovery data

We only access data you explicitly authorize through the OAuth consent flow.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

Your data is used solely to:
- Display your health and fitness metrics within the application
- Generate personal insights and visualizations for your use

We do NOT:
- Sell your data
- Share your data with advertisers
- Use your data for purposes beyond the stated functionality

3. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

- All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest
- We implement industry-standard security practices
- Access is restricted to authenticated users only
- We retain data only as long as needed to provide the service

4. DATA SHARING

Your data is not shared with third parties except:
- With your explicit consent
- When required by law
- With service providers under confidentiality agreements solely to operate the application

5. YOUR RIGHTS AND CONTROLS

You may at any time:
- Revoke access through your Oura or Garmin account settings
- Request a copy of your data
- Request deletion of your data from our systems

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

6. THIRD-PARTY POLICIES

Our application integrates with services governed by their own privacy policies:
- Oura: https://ouraring.com/privacy-policy
- Garmin: https://www.garmin.com/privacy

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

This application is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. POLICY UPDATES

We will update this policy as needed. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.
